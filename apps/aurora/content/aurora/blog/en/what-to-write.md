---
title: What to write when nothing happened
date: 2026-08-20
excerpt: Most days do not have a story in them, and waiting for one is how journals die. Here is what to write on the days that were just days.
---

## The uneventful day is the one worth keeping

A journal kept only on remarkable days is a record of holidays and crises. The ordinary Tuesday
is what you will actually want back in five years — what the flat sounded like, what you were
worrying about, what you ate without thinking.

## Write the specific, not the summary

"Busy day" holds nothing. "Third coffee by eleven, still stuck on the same paragraph" puts you
back in the room. Specificity costs no extra words; it just requires picking a real detail over
a general one.

## Two sentences is a complete entry

The failure mode of journalling is deciding an entry has to be good. It does not. Two sentences
written every day beats a page written twice a month, because the value is in the continuity —
a run of ordinary entries is what makes any single one findable and worth reading later.
